# Brand

## What Common Origin is

Common Origin is the personal creative studio of one person: a music producer, digital designer, graphic designer, product designer, front-end developer, and creative coder, with 15+ years of practice across all of them.

The name is literal. Music, visual design, generative art, software, and writing all share a *common origin*: one person, many forms.

> "Personal creativity has many forms but one origin."

It is not a commercial brand optimising for growth, followers, or revenue. It exists for creative freedom and ownership. That authenticity is the foundation: what the system produces should feel considered and intentional — not trying to impress, not performing ambition.

**Tagline:** A creative studio.

## Origin story

After more than 15 years as a producer — signed releases, international touring, deep involvement in electronic music — starting a family changed the priorities. The intent became full creative control and full ownership of output. Alongside that ran 15+ years of design practice. Common Origin holds both: the intersection of music, visual art, code, and design, released without external pressure, timeline, or expectation.

## Philosophy

From the original brand brief:

- **Entities with multiple parts making a whole** — the brand is a union of disciplines.
- **Cell reproduction, evolution, overlapping layers** — growth from a single origin point.
- **Everything is connected** — universality through shared origin.
- **The intersection is the point** — meaning is made where different things meet.
- **Multiple forms, one origin** — the central idea.

## The logo

Two overlapping solid circles — an asymmetric Venn diagram.

- The left circle is slightly smaller and partly cropped, so the mark reads left to right: origin to now.
- Solid fills only: no outlines, gradients, or decoration.
- The wordmark sits in white inside the overlap: meaning lives at the intersection.
- The wordmark typeface is a heavy, rounded geometric sans — warmer than Inter. It is part of the logo, not the component system.
- Works from ~16px (circles only) to the full lockup.
- **Colour:** the logo uses pure black `#000000` and pure white. It is the only place pure black appears; UI uses near-black `#212529` so the logo stays distinct.

The logo communicates mathematical precision, intersection and connection, directional movement, and human warmth within precision.

## What Common Origin is not

- **Not a SaaS product** — no growth target, no funnel.
- **Not a corporate identity** — no committee, no brand-by-consensus.
- **Not minimalism as fashion** — minimal because everything earns its place.
- **Not imitating other studios** — reference systems set the quality bar, not the identity.
