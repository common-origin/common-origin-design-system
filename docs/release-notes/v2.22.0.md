**Changes to note.** Nothing is removed or renamed, but the palette retune and the status pattern change how many components look:

- **Warning is yellow, not orange** (decision 0043). Warning text, icons, borders and tints use the new warning ramp, and orange now means serious. [#294](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/294)
- **Neutral fills, dividers and input edges moved onto the grey ramp** (decision 0040):
  - neutral fills and the default Chip are lighter
  - dividers are slightly darker (1.53:1)
  - a resting input edge is 3.19:1, and a hovered one 6.01:1
  - disabled text is fainter (1.53:1)
  - the ProgressBar and Slider track is lighter

  [#291](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/291), [#297](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/297)
- **Solid buttons get lighter on hover and press.** The primary, accent and danger Buttons, IconButton and the emphasis Chip mix white in at 8% and 12% (`color-mix()`, decision 0048). Accent text keeps one colour in every state.
- **Emphasis is no longer a darker text colour** (decision 0048). `text.emphasis`, `icon.emphasis` and `border.emphasis` are deprecated aliases of their defaults, and Typography's and Icon's `emphasis` props render as default.
- **Category labels use four display colours plus grey** (decision 0043): `olive`, `periwinkle`, `rose`, `magenta` and `gray`. CategoryLabel's old colour names still work as deprecated aliases of the nearest one, and the default is now `periwinkle`, which looks the same as the old `blue`. Grey "other" is mid-grey, not near-black. [#295](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/295)
- **The status pattern** (decision 0045) gives StatusLabel, Alert and InlineAlert new status icons. At the small size:
  - StatusLabel shows a shape for completed, failed and processing, and text only for pending, scheduled and cancelled. Pending and scheduled are now neutral grey.
  - InlineAlert shows the status shape.

  [#298](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/298), [#299](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/299)
- **Badge's `error`, `warning` and `success` variants render as `accent`** and are deprecated: a badge means new or unread items, so a status count uses StatusLabel instead. [#300](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/300)
- **TransactionListItem** shows pending and failed as a small StatusLabel beside the merchant, instead of coloured dots. [#300](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/300)
- **AccountCard's trend** is an arrow and the signed value, in the success, danger or muted colour, not a green or red category label. [#295](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/295)
- **MoneyDisplay** shows its sign as text, `+$20.00` or `−$20.00` (a true minus), with the whole amount in the success or danger colour. The circled icons and the wrapper's `aria-label` are gone: the visible signed amount is what screen readers read. Expense amounts in TransactionListItem are now red. [#302](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/302)

**New:**
- `color.data.*` chart colours: positive and negative, four categorical colours and other, a diverging scale and a sequential scale ([#296](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/pull/296))
- the serious and AI role tokens
- `opacity.state.hover` and `opacity.state.pressed`
- the ten `status*` icons
- `CategoryDisplayColor`, `BadgeVariant` and `DeprecatedBadgeVariant` types
- [token documentation](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/blob/main/docs/tokens/README.md) explaining the tiers, the ramps and every semantic category

**Deprecated, removed in 3.0:** the old category colour names and tokens, Badge's status variants, the emphasis colour tokens, `fill.emphasis-hover` and `-pressed`, the accent text and icon `-hover` and `-pressed` tokens, and the unused transaction, breadcrumb-font and tooltip-border tokens.
