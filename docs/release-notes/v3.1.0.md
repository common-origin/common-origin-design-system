**Sidebar navigation.** New components for a product's sidebar: `Tooltip` (atom), `Menu`, `SidebarNavItem`, `SidebarNavSection` and `WorkspaceSwitcher` (molecules), and `SidebarNav`, the first organism. New icons `home` and `sidebar`.

**Rows follow density** ([decision 0051](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/blob/main/docs/foundation/decisions/0051-row-heights-and-dense-targets.md)). The new `size.row` scale sets row heights per density, and `compact` may take targets to 24px on every pointer. ListItem and TransactionListItem keep today's heights at `default`. New tokens: `size.row.*`, `size.1400`, `size.5600`, `size.6400`, `component.sidebarNav.width.*` and `motion.delay.hint`.

**Visible changes:**
- Headings `h1`–`h6` are semibold (600); `display` stays bold ([decision 0050](https://github.com/common-origin/common-origin-design-system/blob/main/docs/foundation/decisions/0050-headings-are-semibold.md)).
- A ListItem with `spacing="compact"` is 48px at `default` density, like every list row (it was 44px).
- Inside a `data-co-density` region, list and transaction rows now shrink or grow, and so does their padding.

**Deprecated, removed in 4.0:**
- `spacing` on `List` and `ListItem`: set density on a region with `data-co-density` instead.
- `component.transactionListItem.minHeight`: use `semantic.size.row.lg`.
- `component.alert.title.fontWeight`: use `semantic.fontWeight.semibold`.
